Medicine Awareness
& Verification

Deep-dive into how PharmaTrack ensures every medicine you take is authentic, safe, and properly understood.

Alternative Medicine Suggestion

Mechanism
Users input prescribed medicine; system identifies chemically equivalent alternatives using active compound matching.
Example
'Dolo 650' unavailable? → Suggests 'Calpol 650' or 'Paracip 650' instantly.
Benefit
Ensures continuity of treatment and reduces multiple pharmacy visits, saving time and reducing health risks from treatment delays.

QR Verification Authentication

Mechanism
Scan unique QR code on medicine packaging via the app — instant cloud verification against the authenticated database.
Info Displayed
Manufacturer details, batch number, manufacturing date, and expiry date — all cryptographically verified.
Why It Matters
Counterfeit drugs are a growing crisis. PharmaTrack gives users the power to verify authenticity in seconds, before consuming any medication.
Case Study

The Critical Need
for QR Verification

Real-World Impact · 2023

A reported incident in 2023 highlighted severe consequences of counterfeit drugs — a patient experienced delayed recovery and adverse effects after consuming a fake antibiotic that passed visual inspection but failed chemical verification.

1

Detection

PharmaTrack detects invalid or duplicated QR codes before the user even opens the packaging — immediate red flag alert.

2

Prevention

Real-time alert system warns users to avoid potentially harmful counterfeit medications and guides them to a legitimate source.

3

Trust Restoration

Reliable authentication mechanism significantly improves medicine safety and rebuilds public trust in pharmaceuticals.

Drug Safety Examples

Real Precautions Powered by PharmaTrack

💊

Ibuprofen

⚠️ Avoid on empty stomach. Caution for kidney conditions. Do not combine with blood thinners. Take with food or milk to reduce gastric irritation.

🩸

Iron + Calcium

⚠️ Do not take together — calcium blocks iron absorption. Recommended spacing: at least 2 hours apart. Take iron with Vitamin C for better absorption.

🧠

Metformin

⚠️ Avoid alcohol while taking. Monitor for lactic acidosis symptoms. Kidney function must be checked regularly during use of this medication.
